# 04 · Three directions

Owners choose well when shown and badly when asked to imagine. Every build therefore starts with three complete homepage renders from the same brief. The owner picks one; it becomes the reference for the whole site. The other two are discarded, never blended. `[stated]` This is how Buyuk (Studio → Lumen) and Peptome (option 1 → A2) were actually chosen.

## What must be identical across the three

- Everything in the brief's locked elements, palette musts/nevers, type family list, shape rules, sector exceptions.
- The real content: real product names, real prices, the real logo, the owner's actual headline claim. Never lorem, never "Product name".
- The forbidden list (00 §9). All three must pass the design gate before they are shown.

## What varies — pick three of these axes per brief, never all

1. **Density**: editorial (fewer, larger blocks) vs catalogue (dense grid, nav bar, search up front). In shop sectors one direction must be the dense one; it is usually chosen. `[stated]` Buyuk chose the denser layout.
2. **Hero construction**: split (type left, media right) vs full-bleed media with type on a panel vs product-first (render large, type small). `[stated]` Peptome chose split.
3. **Panel system**: flat-filled tinted panels vs hairline-bordered white panels vs no panels (rules only).
4. **Type pairing**: two different curated grotesks; or, where the sector grants it, one direction with a weight-500+ serif heading.
5. **Signature colour usage**: restrained (links and one button) vs structural (section grounds, category bar) vs a single dark chapter on a light site. `[stated]` Peptome's accepted direction had a dark product chapter.
6. **Imagery treatment**: cut-out on paper vs photographed on set vs video hero.

Each direction gets a short name (never "Option A/B/C"; use a word that describes it: "Lumen", "Ledger", "Atlas") and one sentence on why it suits the sector. Names help the owner refer back to them. `[stated]` "Lumen" and "A2" stuck.

## Presentation

- Three full homepage renders at desktop and mobile widths, same scroll positions, side by side.
- Each with the brief's palette and type shown as a small specimen strip under it.
- One line per direction: what it leans into. No adjectives like "premium", "clean", "modern".
- The owner picks one, and may say one thing to change ("tidy the fonts and boxes"). That becomes v2 of the chosen direction, not a fourth direction. `[stated]` Peptome: option 1 chosen, then "tidied up with better fonts/colours/boxes" into A2.

## What the engine does with a rejection

- A rejected direction is logged with the owner's words in `reference/rejected.md` under the sector.
- If all three are rejected, the engine does not generate three more at random. It asks one question: "Which of the three was closest, and what was wrong with it?" and regenerates from that answer with one axis changed.
- Rejection words that recur across owners become house rules (09).

## Known winning shapes (from the reference library)

- Shop sectors: dense catalogue direction with search and category bar, cool-tinted panels on white.
- Beauty: split hero, actives grid, one dark product chapter, results, ingredient ledger, trust strip.
- Collectibles: dark navy ground, gold signature used structurally, richer buttons and an icon strip.
- Crypto/AI: few strong sections, one ambient hero animation, product screenshots, no cards.
