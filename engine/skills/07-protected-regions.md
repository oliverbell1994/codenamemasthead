# 07 · Protected regions

Once a site converts, parts of it become sacred. `[stated]` "The site is converting well, so the home-page hero must stay as it is; scared of ruining conversions."

## Defaults

- When a site has taken its first twenty paid orders, or when the owner says it is converting, the engine locks: **homepage hero**, **product page template**, **basket and checkout**.
- The owner can lock any other region in Stage 6 or at any time by naming it.

## How a lock behaves

- A chat request that would touch a locked region is refused with a one-line explanation and an offer: "Unlock the hero for this change?" The unlock applies to that change only and is logged.
- SEO work routes around locks: titles, descriptions, schema, new pages and internal links are permitted; layout and copy inside a locked region are not. `[stated]`
- New URLs may be added; existing URLs are never replaced. `[stated]` Landing pages for ads are new pages, never edits to ranking pages. `[stated]`
- Pages built but not yet approved for public linking (a referral page, a partners page) are **unlinked** regions: they exist, pass gates, and have no inbound links until the owner approves. `[stated]`

## What is never locked

- Admin, emails, dispatch rules, documents, prices and stock. These change often and are the owner's business data.

## Logging

Every unlock, who did it, what changed, and the before/after screenshots at three viewports, kept with the site's version history.
