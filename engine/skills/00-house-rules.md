# 00 · House rules

The global design constraints. Sector playbooks (01) may tighten these or grant a named exception; they may never loosen a rule marked **hard**.

Tags: `[stated]` the founder said this directly. `[inferred]` extrapolated from one or two decisions; verify.

## 1. What "premium" means here

- Premium is **crisp, structured and confident**: a clear grid, firm type, hairline rules, generous but not empty space, and real content in every block. `[stated]` The founder's benchmark for premium is the Buyuk site: "crisp, structured". Airy, thin, editorial treatments were judged "not premium enough" and "basic".
- Premium is **specific**. A premium site names the product, the price, the certificate, the dispatch time. Vagueness reads as cheap. `[inferred]` from accepted copy on Buyuk and Peptome.
- Premium is **not** oversized. `[stated]` The founder dislikes oversized display headings. Hero headlines sit in the 40–64px range on desktop, never 96px+.
- Premium is **not** a mood board. Every colour comes from the logo or the sector; every font from the curated list; every shape from the grid.

## 2. Typography — hard

- Lead with a **refined grotesk**. Accepted on live sites: Hanken Grotesk (headings) + Instrument Sans (body) on Buyuk `[stated]`; Instrument Sans on Regent `[stated]`; Host Grotesk on Solvane `[stated]`; Hanken Grotesk on the Peptome redesign `[stated]`.
- Curated list, free: Hanken Grotesk, Instrument Sans, Host Grotesk, Schibsted Grotesk, Public Sans (body only), Work Sans (body only, weights 400–500), Albert Sans, Onest. `[inferred]` beyond the four accepted ones.
- Curated list, commercial (for Studio-tier clients who license them): Söhne, Neue Haas Grotesk, Suisse Int'l, ABC Diatype, GT America, Aeonik, Founders Grotesk, PP Neue Montreal, Basis Grotesque, Untitled Sans, National 2, Graphik. `[inferred]`
- **Never use as a default** because they read as AI-generated: Inter (as a heading face; body use only inside admin UIs), Geist, Bricolage Grotesque, Space Grotesk, Manrope, Plus Jakarta Sans, DM Sans, Outfit, Poppins, Montserrat, Figtree, Sora, Urbanist, Lexend. `[stated]` for Geist and Bricolage (the founder asked whether they are fonts AI commonly uses, then replaced them); `[inferred]` for the rest by the same test.
- **Serifs** are allowed only where the sector playbook grants them, at weight 500 or above, never thin, never "airy editorial". `[stated]` a serif headline swap was rejected on Buyuk; an airy thin-serif treatment was rejected on Peptome. Candidates when granted: Newsreader (opsz ≥ 24, weight ≥ 500), Source Serif 4, GT Sectra, Tiempos, Signifier, Editorial New, Canela. Never Playfair Display, Instrument Serif, Fraunces, Cormorant at light weights, DM Serif.
- Two families per site maximum. Headings in one, body in one; mono only for data (batch numbers, order numbers) if at all.
- Sentence case for headings. No all-caps headlines; small caps or tracked uppercase for labels only (8–9px, +0.08–0.14em).
- Body 15–17px desktop, line-height 1.5–1.6. Measure 60–75 characters. Left-aligned. No centred paragraphs longer than two lines.
- Tabular figures on prices and data.

## 3. Shape and edge — hard

- **Square corners by default.** `[stated]` the founder rejects rounded curves and edges as AI-looking. Permitted radius: 0 on cards, panels, images, sections; 2–4px on form inputs and buttons only where the sector playbook allows; never above 6px anywhere.
- **No pills** as the default button or tag shape, in any sector. Card Castle used them as the founder's way of adding weight to a client-locked navy ground `[stated]`; a contextual choice, never a sector rule.
- **Hairline rules** (1px, ink at 12–18% opacity) are the primary separator. Not drop shadows, not cards floating on shadows.
- **No gradient blobs, glows, mesh gradients, orbs, or soft radial backgrounds.** Flat colour or photography. `[stated]` ("AI slop" rejections across Solvane, Regent, Card Castle).
- Gradients only on a single element and only when the context earns it (Card Castle's gold-gradient buttons were the founder's answer to a flat design looking plain on a client-locked dark ground `[stated]`); never a sector default.
- **No glassmorphism** except as a named exception (beauty granted a monochrome "frosted glass" buy block). `[stated]`
- Borders are 1px. No 2px+ decorative outlines, no double rules, no dashed decoration.

## 4. Layout and spacing — hard

- One grid per site: 12 columns desktop, 4 mobile, 24px gutters desktop, 16px mobile. Content max-width 1200–1280px; shop grids may run to 1360px.
- Spacing scale: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96. Section padding 64–96px desktop, 48 mobile. **Never** 160px+ "breathing" sections with one centred line in them.
- Density follows sector. Shops are **dense**: search bar, category bar, product grid of 3–4 columns with price and stock visible. `[stated]` the accepted Buyuk "Lumen" direction was chosen for its denser shop layout with search and category bar.
- Heroes carry real content: a specific headline, one line of proof, one primary action, and either a product image or a photograph. Never a centred headline over a gradient with two pill buttons. `[stated]` generic heroes rejected on Solvane.
- No "three-step icon cards" as a section pattern. `[stated]` rejected on Solvane. Process steps are shown as a numbered list with hairline rules, or as a horizontal stepper with real screenshots.
- Panels and cards are square, hairline-bordered or flat-filled, and aligned to the grid. A card/panel system is accepted when structured. `[stated]` Peptome A2 card/panel system accepted.
- Trust strips ("as seen in", certifications, payment marks) are allowed and were accepted on Peptome. `[stated]` Keep them single-row, monochrome logos, hairline-ruled.

## 5. Colour

- Ink is never pure black: use a warm or cool near-black (#141414 to #1A1917 range). Paper is never pure white on light sites unless the sector is clinical (clinical may use #FFFFFF with cool-tinted panels). `[inferred]`
- One signature colour per site, derived from the logo or the sector. Everything else is neutrals.
- Light or dark is a sector decision, not a taste decision: peptides/clinical are light `[stated]` ("peptide businesses should not be dark themed"); beauty is black, white and grey with no metallic accent `[stated]`; crypto/AI may be dark but must avoid neon-on-dark and purple-blue; collectibles default to the house rules (Card Castle's blue background and logo were the client's only locks; the founder dislikes the blue `[stated]`).
- **Never**: purple-to-blue gradients, neon accents on dark, rainbow or multi-hue palettes, "AI purple" (#7C3AED family) as a brand colour, gold or copper on a sector that has not granted it. `[stated]` for the Peptome metallic ban; `[inferred]` for the rest.
- Contrast AA minimum on all text pairs. A gate checks this.

## 6. Imagery

- Photography or photographic renders, never drawn illustration where a product is expected. `[stated]` drawn vials were rejected by the founder's team; photographic blue-cap renders were adopted for every product.
- Product proportions must match the real object. `[stated]` vials proportioned like the reference, "not boxy".
- One camera angle and one lighting setup per product family across the site.
- No AI-generated people, no stock handshake photos, no abstract 3D blobs, no "futuristic" renders of nothing.
- Placeholders are honest: neutral blocks at the correct aspect ratio, labelled, never lorem-style stock. Real imagery drops in later without a rebuild. `[stated]` placeholders accepted on Peptome ahead of real photography.
- Images must render correctly on every device; a gate checks three viewports. `[stated]` standing instruction.
- Logos are never redrawn unless the owner unlocks them. `[stated]` Peptome logo is on packaging and must never change.

## 7. Copy

- No "Welcome to", "Elevate", "Unlock", "Seamless", "Empower", "Discover the power of", "Your journey", "Next-level", "Revolutionise". No exclamation marks. No emoji anywhere on a customer-facing page.
- Headlines are specific nouns and claims: what it is, who it is for, what proves it.
- Sector language rules override everything else (e.g. laboratory framing on research supplies; see 01). `[stated]`
- Credits to a team name rather than invented personal authors where the owner prefers it. `[stated]` ("BuyUK Peptides Scientific Team"). No invented testimonials, ever.

## 8. Motion

- Default: none beyond hover states and 150–200ms transitions.
- A sector may grant one ambient animation behind the hero (crypto/AI granted). `[stated]` Solvane asked for animation behind the hero. Never parallax everywhere, never scroll-jacking, never floating icons.

## 9. The forbidden list (gate-checked)

A build fails the design gate if it contains any of: border-radius above 6px on a non-input element; any CSS gradient outside a sector-granted exception; backdrop-filter outside a sector-granted exception; any font not on the curated list; box-shadow with blur above 8px; a hero without an image or product; a "three icon cards" section; headline font-size above 72px; centred body paragraphs over two lines; emoji in markup; any of the banned phrases in copy; purple-blue hue pairs in the palette; pill buttons on a sector that has not granted them.

## 10. Whose taste is this? — hard

- House rules encode the **founder's** taste and the platform's standard. A client's wishes are **site-level locks**, honoured on that site and never promoted to a house or sector rule. `[stated]` Card Castle: the client locked the blue background and the logo; the founder dislikes the blue.
- Decisions the founder makes *inside* a client's locks (how to make a locked navy look less plain) are **contextual**: filed with the condition that produced them, never as unconditional rules.
- When a client asks for something outside the house style (a gradient button, pills, a colour the sector would not choose), the engine does it, says in one line that it sits outside the house style, and records it as a client lock.
- A client's build therefore never enters `reference/accepted.md` as founder taste; it is filed under client-directed.

## 11. How to judge, when in doubt

Ask: would this look at home next to the accepted Buyuk and Peptome homepages, and would it look like a £20k agency built it? If the honest answer is "it looks like a template" or "it looks generated", it fails, whatever the rules above say. The rules exist to make that judgement automatic, not to replace it.
