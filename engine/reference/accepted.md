# Reference · Accepted

What the founder said yes to, by site, with the words where they exist. The live sites are the visual references; this file records the decisions.

## Buyuk Peptides (sector A) — buyukpeptides.com

- Direction "Lumen": white, cool-blue tinted tiles, navy ink, denser shop layout with search and category bar. Chosen as "the most premium". Outcome: "the site is converting well."
- Type: Hanken Grotesk headings + Instrument Sans body. Kept when a serif swap was offered.
- Imagery: photographic blue-cap vial renders for every product and size, proportioned like the reference supplier's vials.
- Trust: WhatsApp button; certificates per product/batch; "Scientific Team" byline; no lab named; no people section. Shop: fewest clicks to checkout; no postcode autofill; card + crypto and instant bank gateways; hero frozen once converting.

## Peptome (sector B) — peptomeskin.com

- Direction "option 1": split hero (type left, photo right), actives grid, dark product chapter, results, ingredient ledger; tidied into "A2" (Hanken Grotesk, card/panel system, "as seen in" strip). Founder: "really like it", "the content flow is good."
- Palette: black, white, greys; mono "frosted glass" buy block.
- Rules kept: logo untouched; no price or add-to-basket in the hero; placeholders accepted ahead of real photography; video hero allowed.
- USP led by the active ingredient (GHK-Cu) as the headline claim.

## Card Castle Dubai (sector C) — tcgdubai.com — client-locked ground

- Client locks: the blue background and the logo, nothing else. `[stated]` Within that, the founder chose the richer treatment (gradient gold buttons with icons, icon strip, pills) because the flat version "looked a bit plain" on the dark ground. `[stated]` Founder's view of the whole: "I hate the blue." Filed as a contextual decision (what to do on a client-locked dark ground), not as taste.

## Mesh (sector D) — mesh-network.ai

- The founder's bar for "T1" in this sector: clean, simple, premium, nothing that looks generated, no oversized headings.

## Solvane (sector D) — solvane.ai

- Host Grotesk chosen after rejecting AI-common fonts; animation behind the hero accepted as the fix for a generic hero; must differ completely from Mesh.

## Regent Peptides (sector A) — regentpeptides.com

- Refined-grotesk type (Instrument Sans) chosen over a serif headline; existing colours kept while making it premium and less AI (fonts, box sizes). Site later paused; the type decision stands.

## Peptidarium (sector A, brief stage)

- Lab-style direction with red-cap vials and a red signature colour, "pushed further towards a lab look"; wiki/library structure with a store that converts; must differ completely from Buyuk and BPL; SEO drives everything.

## Cross-site patterns the engine should treat as accepted

- Refined grotesk type, structured panels, hairline rules, dense shops; photographic renders with a signature element (cap colour); real trust content over decoration; placeholders first and a password screen until real imagery is in; the same stack behind every site (static storefront on an edge host, Medusa on managed infra, Cloudflare, object storage, email API, GitHub).
